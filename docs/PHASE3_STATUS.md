# Phase 3 — signed atomic settlement (local gate complete)

The supported **manual, vanilla-limit-order local-EVM gate** passes: actual signed orders → funded atomic fill → canonical position/funding → partial/full close → settled owner token withdrawals. This is not a public deployment or permission to enable Fuji. Local fixtures and receipts are not public trades, liquidity or profitability evidence.

## Implementation and explicit limits

- [Canonical vault ledger](COLLATERAL_VAULT.md): exact-delta collateral, immutable cap, owner-only withdrawals, reservations, bilateral pair margins, opening fees and insurance in one conserved liability ledger. Only one bootstrap-bound settlement module may mutate those buckets; code and exact vault/deployment references are checked before local deposits, and rebinding is impossible. A bound module is a critical trusted contract, not an unsigned matcher plugin.
- `contracts/evm/src/AvensSettlement.sol`: EIP-712 `Avensdex`/`1`, current chain ID and settlement address, with the exact [Phase 2 signed schema](CLOB_ADMISSION.md). Both EOA signatures, low-s encoding, positive nonce, trader identity, expiry, nonce-to-hash binding and cumulative fills are enforced atomically with vault collateral. EIP-1271/smart-account signatures are unsupported.
- Supported orders are manual limit orders with all flags false. Market, reduce-only, post-only, IOC and strategy settlement are explicitly rejected; no spot-only exchange, delegated strategy authority or simulated success is substituted. Strategy allocation/inventory/policy buckets are deferred to Phase 5 and must extend the same canonical ledger before they can be enabled.
- Market ID is 1–8, size6 ≤1e15, price18 ≤1e27, leverage 1–10, signed fee cap 3–10,000 bps, expiry strictly future and at most one day ahead, and each matched notional is at least 1 collateral unit (1e6). Opening fees are rounded-up 3 bps **per fill**. Margin is rounded up from execution notional/leverage. Fill price must satisfy both signed limits and be within 1% of a fresh independent oracle reference. Neither side may open already unsafe at the 500-bps maintenance threshold. Each owner has at most 32 active bilateral pairs; a partial fill creates a distinct pair.
- Matching is permissionless but signatures/risk/collateral are not bypassable. Pair IDs are permanently unique. An unfunded side, invalid signature/nonce, conflicting payload, overfill, stale source or failed vault transition rolls back both sides, fill counters, fees and position metadata. Offchain acknowledgments do not establish cancellation or settlement.
- `reserveOrder(order, signature, amount)` is owner-only and bounds optional escrow by remaining signed order margin plus fee at the limit price. Matching consumes that owner's reservation then free balance. Full fills release residual reservation. A released reservation is not itself an order cancellation.
- Exact-hash cancellation is separate from broad nonce invalidation. Known orders can be cancelled directly by their owner or through an owner-submitted EIP-712 Cancel. `cancelSignedOrder(originalOrder, originalSignature)` supports owner cancellation before the first onchain reservation/fill while proving the original order identity. Cancelling one hash does **not** advance the owner's nonce floor. Only explicit `invalidateNonce(n)` blocks all nonces ≤n; its floor never decreases. Invalidated reservation escrow remains recoverable through owner cancellation, and expired signed orders can be released permissionlessly. Signed-cancel relaying by a non-owner is unsupported.
- [Independent oracle/funding boundary](SETTLEMENT_ORACLE.md): separate immutable local publishers, original source timestamps, bounded rates, explicit rate expiry and checkpointed cumulative index without expired-gap backfill. This trusted-publisher adapter is not a qualified public oracle or the Phase 4 adaptive controller.
- Permissionless `settleFunding(pairId)` applies the independent index to canonical pair margins with payer clipping, fractional carry and same-index idempotency. Funding settles before any close/liquidation. Either recorded pair owner can close partially or fully at the fresh independent oracle price; PnL is bounded by that pair's collateral, never other owners' free funds. Partial closes retain remaining margin/remainder dust. Fees stay locked in the canonical fee bucket; no fee-distribution/sweep authority exists.
- Permissionless liquidation requires actual unsafe capped equity at the fresh reference after funding. A penalty of at most 1% of current notional is taken only from the selected unsafe side's remaining capped equity; half (rounded down) credits the liquidator and the rest insurance. Unrelated balances and winner payouts do not fund a liquidation shortfall. No guaranteed keeper reward is claimed when the unsafe side has no remaining equity.
- [Durable reconciliation](SETTLEMENT_RECONCILIATION.md) persists intent identity before submission, records known transaction/replacement candidates separately, checks actual decoded contract events and canonical block/confirmation identity, and freezes unknown/reverted/reorged outcomes. Restart never treats history or a missing submission hash as permission to retry. There is no automatic signing/broadcast/retry path. Canonical outcomes are not yet published back into the matcher command journal/feed or used to release its provisional-match capacity; that public transport/status integration remains explicit Phase 4 work. The local gate verifies the separate contract/reconciler/position boundaries without silently enabling the service.
- `readCanonicalPosition` reads settlement metadata and vault margins at one pinned canonical block, rechecks both providers' block identity, distinguishes never-used pair IDs from closed positions and refuses mismatched ledgers. Fill receipts are history, not another current-position or spendable-balance ledger.

## Reproduce

Pinned Node/npm, Foundry/Solidity and Rust toolchains are listed in [foundation checks](FOUNDATION_CHECKS.md).

```sh
npm ci --ignore-scripts
npm test
npm run test:funding:rust
forge fmt --root contracts/evm --check
forge test --root contracts/evm --match-path test/AvensSettlement.t.sol --gas-report
```

`npm test` includes `npm run test:settlement:evm`, which builds tracked contracts and spawns a **fresh loopback-only Anvil/Paris chain 31337**. It generates ephemeral development keys, funds only those fixture addresses and cannot target a configurable public RPC. The pure matcher produces a provisional match from those wallet-signed orders; the exact matched hashes/size/price are submitted to the contract. It does not reset an existing funded node or source-chain state. The run checks 24 actual local EVM receipts, ABI-compatible TS/Solidity order hashes, signed partial settlement, confirmation delay, receipt decoding, journal restart/canonical revalidation, genuine position reads, bounded funding, partial/full closes and both owners' exact token withdrawals. Remaining custody equals the locked opening-fee liability; escrow, free and pair margins reconcile to zero. Temporary keys/node/journal are discarded; no generated addresses/receipts are presented as public evidence.

Current full suite: typecheck, **94 TypeScript tests**, **12 Python tests**, **84 Foundry tests** (1,000 runs per configured fuzz property), plus the fresh local Anvil lifecycle. The four locked Rust reference tests also pass; `npm audit --audit-level=high` reports zero root dependency vulnerabilities. These do not audit the ignored legacy frontend or establish public readiness. The 29 settlement tests include forgery/domain/high-s/field tampering, cancellation scope/races, invalidation recovery, conflicting nonce/replay, insufficient-side atomic rollback, unsupported flags, source/rate expiry, both funding signs, payer clipping/remainders, healthy/unhealthy liquidation, partial dust, conservation, position caps and fixed-owner payouts.

A clean tracked-source `git archive` of **`bfdcbec`** independently passed `npm ci --ignore-scripts`, the full suite including Anvil, the four locked Rust tests and Solidity formatting outside the working tree. It required no ignored source snapshot, frontend dependencies or configured wallet secrets. Hosted Phase 3 CI status was not checked; local/clean results do not establish a hosted run.

Pinned optimized Paris runtime sizes: vault **9,182 bytes**, oracle **2,802 bytes**, settlement **19,197 bytes**, below EIP-170's 24,576-byte limit. Tests assert local fill gas ≤3,000,000, full-close gas ≤1,500,000 and liquidation gas ≤2,000,000 for the bounded fixture paths. Gas-report observed maxima in this local test run: match 607,625, close 184,672, liquidation 205,498 gas. These are tested local observations/bounds, not a universal gas proof, Fuji fee quote or Avalanche finality measurement.

OpenCode drafted the settlement contract/tests and journal/tests. Pi integration reviewed the trust boundary, fixed cancellation-floor scope and restart/retry/confirmation issues, added funding checkpoints and pinned canonical position reads, and verified the real local EVM lifecycle. This is not an independent security audit.

## Public and later-phase gates remain closed

Fuji deposits, price/rate publication and settlement mutations cannot activate in these versions. Mainnet is rejected; the manifest remains execution-disabled and HTTP writes/readiness stay 503. An address, matching signature, local test result or observed code hash cannot flip those gates.

Independent asset/oracle/venue/canonicality qualification, adaptive funding/mark/epoch integration, the existing terminal's genuine wallet/browser lifecycle, Guardian/Autopilot strategy authority and both-leg execution, redistribution rights, independent security review and bounded public signing/deployment authorization remain Phase 1/4/5/6 work. No public lifecycle, live hedge, yield, liquidity or release-readiness claim is made.
